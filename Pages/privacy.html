Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 17 August 2026. Your trust matters to us — here is exactly how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Silicon Properties Kenya ("Silicon Properties," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through siliconpropertieske.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, and message content when you fill out a contact form, enquire about a listing, or reach out to us via WhatsApp.
  • Automatically collected information — such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and general location, gathered through standard web server logs and analytics tools.
  • Communication records — records of calls, emails, or WhatsApp messages exchanged with our team when you enquire about a property.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your property enquiries and provide the information, viewings, or documentation you request;
  • Communicate with you about listings, offers, or updates that may interest you;
  • Improve our Site, listings, and overall service based on how visitors use them;
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the Site; and
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our Site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how the Site is used, and support features such as the language switcher. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some Site functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site integrates a small number of third-party services to operate properly, each of which has its own privacy practices:

  • WhatsApp Business — used so you can message our team directly; conversations are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Google Maps & Google Translate — used to display our office location and offer multilingual browsing.
  • Social media links — our Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn pages are governed by those platforms' respective privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties, as we do not control how they process data.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information with property sellers, landlords, or partner agents strictly where necessary to facilitate an enquiry or transaction you have initiated, and with service providers who help us operate the Site (such as hosting and communication tools). We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are intended for adults seeking real estate services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
